More than a drafting studio

TEXOSPEC is a 360° fashion incubation and brand venture studio. We join creativity and technical precision to support founders, startups and established brands with end-to-end solutions that turn ideas into scalable, market-ready fashion labels.

Our in-house team delivers exact-fit digital patterns, size grading, tech packs and marker plans that meet industry standards and production expectations.

Our vision

To make Kerala a globally recognised fashion design powerhouse, with a metropolitan-grade ecosystem that connects home-grown creative talent directly to national and international markets.

Our mission

  • Help fashion graduates grow into respected design professionals.
  • Train designers in digital CAD, 3D prototyping and factory-standard specifications.
  • Build a collaborative community of designers.
  • Mentor independent labels from concept to viable brand.

The name and the mark

Texo comes from the Latin root for weaving and constructing fabric. Spec stands for technical specifications: millimetre accuracy and factory-ready blueprints.

The faceted TS monogram shows a flat 2D panel folding into a 3D garment, with the T anchoring into the S like a secure seam.

Services for every stage of a collection

Choose a single service or work with us across the full journey, from legal setup to launch.

Brand identity and legal setup

Company registration, trademark filing (Class 25 and 40), GST and compliance, plus brand naming, logo and identity guidelines.

CAD pattern engineering

2D and 3D pattern drafting, precision grading across sizes, fit correction and tolerance optimisation in Seamly2D and CLO 3D.

Factory-ready tech packs

Vector flats, points of measure, size charts, stitch specifications and a full bill of materials, delivered as outlined multi-page PDFs.

3D virtual sampling

Fit and drape simulation on 3D avatars before fabric is cut, with photorealistic renders for buyers, lookbooks and pre-orders.

Manufacturing and packaging

Fabric and trim sourcing, fit samples, supervised bulk production with partner factories, and custom labels, tags and retail packaging.

Launch and digital growth

Online store setup, launch strategy, social positioning, lookbooks, influencer collaborations and performance marketing.

Custom graphic tee design

Illustrations and typography for streetwear and D2C collections, delivered as print-ready 300 DPI or vector files with Pantone Textile matching and 1:1 placement maps for DTF, DTG, screen and puff printing.

What you receive

Every project ends with a complete technical package you can take to any domestic or overseas manufacturer.

Graded pattern chart

  • Master patterns in DXF, ASTM/AAMA and 1:1 PDF
  • Proportional grading from XS to 3XL+
  • Marker planning and yield optimisation
  • Seam allowances, notches, grainlines and drill marks

Design specification chart

  • Full points-of-measure matrix
  • Plus/minus tolerance limits for quality audits
  • Stitches per inch, seam classes and thread tension
  • Millimetre placement of artwork, labels and trims

Detailed cost sheet

  • Fabric consumption and yield modelling
  • Bill of materials valuation
  • Cut, make and trim projections
  • Target FOB cost and margin planning

Files are calibrated in sRGB for screen review and in CMYK with Pantone Textile (TCX/TPX) for dyeing and printing.

Why brands choose TEXOSPEC

Apparel brands often face high sample rejection, miscommunication with cut-and-sew factories and wasted fabric, usually because the technical specification was incomplete. We remove that bottleneck.

Meaningful careers in Kerala

We give qualified fashion graduates technical roles such as flat drafter, specification engineer and 3D apparel technician, with continuing training in vector drafting, grading and 3D simulation.

  • Precise blueprints, not vague sketches

    Exact stitch specs, seam allowances and measurements that factories can execute without guesswork.

  • Fewer physical sample rounds

    3D fit simulation validates drape and fit before a single metre of fabric is cut.

  • Lower cost, less waste

    Virtual drape analysis and an exact bill of materials reduce scrap and production cost.

  • Speaks the factory's language

    Outlined-font PDFs, POM charts and BOM breakdowns built for industrial cut-and-sew production.
